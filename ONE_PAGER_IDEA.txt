THE POST-IAM CONTEXT GAP
------------------------
Identity systems (IAM, OIDC, OAuth) establish who an agent or service principal is when a request starts. The harder failure happens one step later: an application retrieval bug, shared vector store misconfiguration, or session-summary mixup returns records belonging to another tenant or a revoked policy epoch and assembles them into a single LLM prompt. Once foreign bytes enter model context, prompt instructions alone cannot enforce tenant boundaries.
