WHY THIS TEAM FITS POST-IAM INFERENCE GOVERNANCE
------------------------------------------------
Most teams approaching AI safety or enterprise governance understand only one side of the stack:

1. Identity and API-gateway builders understand principals, OAuth/OIDC tokens, and policy epochs, yet treat the LLM runtime and KV cache as an opaque stateless black box.
2. Inference-engine engineers understand paged KV block tables, scheduler batching, and attention kernels, yet rarely model multi-tenant enterprise authority, mid-flight policy revocation, or payload-free audit receipts.

Vervain sits directly at the seam between those two worlds:
- From enterprise IAM experience at Okta, the architecture treats server-owned identity and native datastore permissions as authoritative, rejects client-supplied authority overrides, pairs every enforcement claim with explicit denominators and exclusions, and keeps protected raw text out of audit logs and receipts.
- From hands-on GPU inference systems work, the roadmap carries that exact same server-owned scope tag down from hosted provider envelopes (today's Google Gemini context boundary) into physical paged-KV block ownership and pre-softmax scope masking for self-hosted open-weight deployments.
